# Improving Indoor Air Quality in Offices

Indoor air quality matters more than most people think. Studies show that people spend around 90% of their time indoors, and the air inside buildings can sometimes be worse than the air outside. Poor air quality in the office has been linked to tiredness, headaches, and lower productivity, according to experts.

There are many things that can affect the air in a workplace, such as ventilation, cleaning products, office equipment, and even the building materials themselves. Improving ventilation is often recommended as a first step, and some companies also use air purifiers or add plants to their spaces.

Every office is different, so results can vary. It is generally a good idea to look into the options and see what makes sense for your workplace before spending money on equipment.
