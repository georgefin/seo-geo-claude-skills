# Air Conditioning Maintenance in Athens

Keeping your air conditioner in good shape is important, especially before the summer season starts. Our company, Atlas Climate Care Ltd., has been servicing air conditioning units across Athens and the wider Attica region since 2011. Our maintenance visits include cleaning the filters, checking the refrigerant pressure, and testing the unit's overall performance.

A well-maintained unit runs more efficiently and lasts longer. Most manufacturers recommend a professional service at least once a year. If you have noticed reduced cooling performance, unusual noises, or higher electricity bills, it may be time to book a maintenance visit.

Contact our team to schedule an appointment at a time that suits you.
