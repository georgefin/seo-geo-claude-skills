**How to Descale an Espresso Machine at Home**

By Priya Shah · Published 14 February 2026

Limescale is the most common reason a home espresso machine loses pressure, runs
slowly, and starts to taste bitter. Descaling dissolves that mineral build-up. The
whole job takes about 25 minutes with ingredients you may already have.

We use plain food-grade citric acid: 2 tablespoons dissolved in 1 litre of warm
water. (We stock food-grade citric acid in the shop — £4.50 for a 250g tub, enough
for about six descales.)

### The five steps

1. Empty the water tank and fill it with the citric acid solution.
2. Run a third of the tank through the group head, then switch off and let it sit
   for 15 minutes.
3. Run the rest of the solution through the group head and the steam wand.
4. Rinse: run two full tanks of fresh water through the machine.
5. Brew and discard one espresso to clear any residual taste.

How often? For most tap water, descale every three months. If you use bottled or
filtered water you can stretch the interval, but do not skip it entirely.

This method suits most home pump machines. If your machine has a built-in automatic
cleaning programme, follow the manufacturer's instructions instead.
