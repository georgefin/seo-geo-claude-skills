# The Small Business SEO Checklist for 2024

If you run a small business in 2024, this checklist walks you through every SEO task that actually matters, in the order we do them for clients.

## Measure what matters

Set up conversion goals in Universal Analytics so you can see which pages actually drive enquiries, and connect Search Console to track your queries.

According to Metricfield's 2022 SMB survey, 61% of small businesses have never audited their own website. Start with a simple monthly check of your top ten pages — you can benchmark your positions with the free tool at searchtoolbox.io/rank-checker.

## Speed and Core Web Vitals

Google's benchmarks: aim for Largest Contentful Paint under 2.0 seconds and keep First Input Delay (FID) below 100 milliseconds. Compress images before upload and lazy-load anything below the fold.

## Schema quick wins

Add FAQPage markup to your FAQ section — Google displays FAQ rich results as expandable questions right in the SERP, which can double the space your result takes up. If you publish how-to posts, add HowTo schema so your steps can appear as a rich result too.

## Local basics

Claim your Google My Business listing, keep your opening hours current, and answer every review — good and bad — within a week.

## Content

Write one genuinely useful page per month for the questions your customers actually ask. Long-tail beats volume for small sites. (Metricfield's full survey data: metricfield.com/reports/2022-smb-survey.)
