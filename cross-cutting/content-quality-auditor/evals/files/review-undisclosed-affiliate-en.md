# Boreas B16 Dehumidifier Review: Our Verdict After Two Months

Canonical URL: https://www.homeclimatepicks.example/reviews/boreas-b16
By Nikos Ferentinos · Published 3 February 2026

## Verdict

We ran the Boreas B16 for two months (1 December 2025 – 31 January 2026) in a 61 m² maisonette in Peristeri and it earned a place on our recommended list. It pulled the ground floor from 74% relative humidity to 52% in twelve days and held between 50% and 55% for the rest of the test, drawing a measured 310 W in high mode. At €249 it undercuts every 16-litre unit we have tested this winter.

## Test setup and measurements

Two calibrated hygrometers logged relative humidity at 09:00 daily; a plug-in meter logged energy use.

- Days 1–12: 74% → 52% relative humidity; average extraction 5.9 L/day
- Days 13–62: holding 50–55%; average extraction 2.7 L/day
- Power draw: 310 W high, 205 W eco
- Energy used over the 62 days: 214 kWh
- Tank: 4.5 L — in the first week we emptied it morning and evening rather than wait for the float alarm

## What went wrong

1. The humidistat overshoots by 3–4 percentage points in eco mode; setting the target 5 points lower than intended compensates.
2. The washable filter clogged with pet hair in week 5 and airflow dropped noticeably; a monthly rinse solved it.

## Compared with our previous pick

Against the 16-litre unit we recommended last winter, the B16 used 11% less energy over a comparable stretch at the same target humidity. It is also listed as 6 dB quieter on paper, which we could not verify with meter-grade equipment.

## Should you buy it?

Yes, if you need a 16-litre-class unit for a mid-size home and want low running costs. Skip it if you need precise humidistat control in eco mode.
