# How Much Does Air Conditioning Maintenance Cost in Athens? (2026 Prices)

Canonical URL: https://www.attikiserviceguides.example/ac-maintenance-cost-athens
By the Attiki Service Guides editorial team · Updated 20 July 2026

In Athens in 2026, a standard maintenance visit for one residential split-type air conditioner costs €35–€45 including VAT. The price typically covers filter cleaning, indoor and outdoor coil cleaning, a refrigerant pressure check, and a performance test. Multi-unit discounts of 10–15% are common from the second unit onward.

## What a standard visit includes

A standard visit takes 45–60 minutes per unit and covers:

- Washing or replacing the filters
- Chemical cleaning of the indoor unit's coil and blower
- Cleaning the outdoor unit's condenser coil
- A refrigerant pressure check
- A drain-line flush and a performance test

Per the manufacturer guidance collected in the EPEE maintenance note, annual professional cleaning keeps rated efficiency within 5% of factory spec (source: https://epee-guidance.example.org/split-maintenance).

## What changes the price

- Accessibility: outdoor units on facades or flat roofs add €10–€20 for safe access
- Unit condition: heavily fouled coils can add 30–45 minutes of labor
- Refrigerant top-up: billed separately, typically €25–€40 per visit when needed

## Is the visit worth the money?

A clean unit draws less electricity for the same cooling. The EPEE note linked above puts professionally maintained units within 5% of factory efficiency, while neglected coils drift further from spec each season. Several manufacturers also make annual professional maintenance a condition of their extended-warranty terms, so keep every service receipt with the unit's paperwork.

## Budgeting for the year

For a standard single-split maintenance visit in Athens, expect to pay €80–€120 including VAT. On top of that, set aside a small reserve for parts such as capacitors (€15–€30 fitted). Booking in March or April, before the first heatwave, usually shortens the wait from ten days to two or three.
