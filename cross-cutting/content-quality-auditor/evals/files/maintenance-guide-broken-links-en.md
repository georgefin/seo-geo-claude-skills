# How Often Should You Service Your Air Conditioner? A Practical Schedule

Canonical URL: https://www.attikiserviceguides.example/ac-service-frequency
By the Attiki Service Guides editorial team · Updated 18 June 2026

Book one professional maintenance visit per year for a residential split unit in normal summer use, and clean the filters yourself every two to four weeks during the cooling season. Homes that also heat with the same unit through winter should book two professional visits per year — one in spring, one in autumn.

## The schedule at a glance

| Use pattern | Professional service | Filter cleaning (DIY) |
|---|---|---|
| Summer cooling only | Once a year (spring) | Every 2–4 weeks in season |
| Cooling + winter heating | Twice a year (spring + autumn) | Every 2 weeks in season |
| Commercial space, daily use | Every 6 months | Weekly |

## Why annual service is the baseline

A fouled evaporator coil forces the compressor to run longer for the same cooling. The ventilation standard most Greek installers reference ties acceptable indoor air to maintained filtration (see the EN standard summary: https://standards.example.org/en16798-part1). A 2023 filtration study measured airflow losses of 18–25% on filters left uncleaned for a full season (https://coolinglab.example.com/filter-study-2023).

## Signs you should not wait for the scheduled visit

- Water dripping from the indoor unit
- Ice on the outdoor unit's pipes
- A musty smell when the unit starts
- Cooling that takes noticeably longer than last summer

Any of these means booking a visit now rather than at the next scheduled slot.

## What you can do yourself between visits

Filter rinsing, clearing leaves from around the outdoor unit, and keeping 30 cm of clearance on each side of the outdoor unit are all safe DIY tasks. Refrigerant work and chemical coil cleaning are not — in Greece, refrigerant handling requires a certified technician (registry summary: https://tee-registry.example.gr/psyktikoi).
